## Overview

People Growth Africa is the digital home of an HR and organisational consulting practice that helps growing businesses across Nigeria and Africa build the HR systems and cultures that make growth sustainable. The site presents the firm's vision, mission and values, its 15 specialised HR service areas, and an events programme of webinars and mentorship cohorts — all wrapped in a polished, purpose-driven brand experience.

## Key Features

- **15 Specialised Service Areas**: HR Advisory & Consulting, Learning & Development, Organisational Design, Performance Management, HR Audits & Compliance, Recruitment & Talent Management, and more — each with a detailed "View Details" view so visitors can explore the full offering.
- **Events & Webinars**: An auto-scrolling events showcase for upcoming webinars and mentorship cohorts, complete with pause/play controls and one-click free registration links.
- **Member Accounts**: Sign up and log in flows so cohorts and event attendees can manage their participation, plus a prominent "Talk to Us" call to action and WhatsApp contact path.
- **Content & Storytelling**: Dedicated Events and Blog sections alongside Vision, Mission, Target and values pages that communicate the firm's purpose-driven, people-focused positioning.
- **Motion-Rich Interface**: Framer Motion animations — including the sliding events carousel — give the site a lively, premium feel without sacrificing load time.

## Technologies Used

- **Frontend**: React 19, Vite, React Router, Framer Motion, Tailwind CSS 4
- **Analytics & Hosting**: Vercel Analytics, Vercel
- **Dev & Tooling**: ESLint, code-split hashed production bundle for fast first paint
